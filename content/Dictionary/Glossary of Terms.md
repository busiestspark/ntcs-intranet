This page contains a list of important terms used throughout CAIN. These are terms that already exist in the English lexicon and which do not pertain to specific facets of CAIN.

# Glossary

## Meme

*Noun* - An idea or concept that spreads throughout a culture or society. Within CAIN, it generally refers to a packet of information that may induce effects on those that perceive it. Such packets are referred to as "Memetic Agents."

"Memetic" - *Adjective* form - Relating to memes. Something that is memetic usually imitates something else in some way by mimicking the information of the original.

## Mnemonic

*Noun, adjective* - Relating to memory. If something has "mnemonic effects", it means that it affects memory in some way.

## Noosphere

*Noun* - The realm of psychic energy. Currently believed to be an alternate dimension or "plane" comprised of the collective energy of all thinking beings. It is not an entirely physical space and does not necessarily abide by the same laws of nature as our physical reality.

"Noological" - *Adjective* form - Of or relating to the Noosphere. Usually used to refer to physical objects or entities originating from the Noosphere.

See full article [[Noosphere|here.]]

## Psychic

*Adjective* - Relating to the mind. Within CAIN, this is a catch-all term for supernatural phenomenon, as all instances of such are currently believed to be due to the "[[Glossary of Terms#Noosphere|Noosphere]]."
