**Sin Overload** is the state that an exorcist enters when they overuse their powers. It is an enormously painful state that represents the transformation into a new [[Sins|Sin]].

An exorcist may survive Sin Overload through strength of will, partially transforming by manifesting a [[Sin Mark]] instead of becoming a Sin outright.

The Sins born of a Sin Overload are almost always more powerful than their naturally-occurring counterparts due to the experience and mental fortitude that an exorcist possesses. CAIN advises against entering Sin Overload if at all possible.
