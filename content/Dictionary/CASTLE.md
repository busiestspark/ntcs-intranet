**CASTLE** is CAIN's housing and welfare division.

They are responsible for housing, teaching, and generally taking care of registered [[Exorcist|exorcists]]. They are also the main bureaucratic body that handles the day-to-day running of the organization.

As most exorcists are recruited in adolescence, CASTLE has developed a robust education system to keep them sharp and aware of the world beyond the organization.
