**Sin Seeds** are tumorous growths present in the bodies of exorcists. Every exorcist possesses a single Sin Seed in their heart or brain that grants them their psychic abilities.

Little is known about how Sin Seeds function, but they exclusively form in individuals with high concentrations of [[Grace]].

To further their research, TEMERITY will offer a bonus to any exorcists who recover the bodies of their fallen kin.
