**Grace** is a person's inherent attunement to psychic phenomenon. It affects their ability to witness psychic effects and entities without issue, as well as their ability to control them.

While Graceless individuals are capable of witnessing certain psychic phenomenon, they often find the experience disturbing and highly traumatic.

Individuals with high concentrations of Grace are at risk of creating or becoming [[Sins]]; it is CAIN's directive to find such individuals and recruit them as [[Exorcist|Exorcists]] before that happens.
