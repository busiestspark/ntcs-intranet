**AUTHORITY** is the primary governing body of CAIN.

They are responsible for overseeing global CAIN activities and upholding the [[Veil Protocol]].

<div class="redacted">The leadership of AUTHORITY consists of eight elders, chosen by <span>succession</span> and <span>a secret vote</span>. Their true identities are kept secret and they are to be referred to exclusively by their titles.</div>
