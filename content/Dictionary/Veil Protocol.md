The **Veil Protocol** is the oath of secrecy that CAIN upholds. Upholding it means ensuring that CAIN, its operations, and psychic phenomena at large do not become known to the general public.

All CAIN personnel are expected to uphold the Veil Protocol, and intentional subversion or sabotage of it are considered severe offenses.

# Punitive Measures

## CLASS-A Violation

A "CLASS-A" violation occurs when a CAIN employee intentionally violates the Veil Protocol by taking action that is likely to reveal protected information to the general public.

Depending on the severity, such violations may be punishable by the immediate and indefinite revocation of all employee rights and privileges.[^1]

## CLASS-B Violation

A "CLASS-B" violation occurs when a CAIN employee accidentally violates the Veil Protocol. This includes unforeseen consequences of otherwise intentional actions that result in a breach of protocol.

Punishments for such violations are determined on a case-by-case basis by Site Adjudicators based on the severity of the infraction.

[^1]: It has been determined too risky to release terminated individuals back into society even after a complete memory purge. Personnel are expected to remain with the organization permanently for the sake of the protocol.
