**Justice** is a Virtue currently stationed on the [[SERAPH Orbital Rail]].

Unlike other Virtues, Justice has complete freedom of movement throughout CAIN facilities. This is thanks to their Blasphemy, LAW, which restricts them from denying any direct order given by a superior.

He currently holds the record for most successful missions in CAIN history.
