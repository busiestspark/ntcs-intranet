# Overview

Violet Okonoi is currently operating as a Junior Exorcist in the North American Division.

She is generally dour and aloof, but exhibits a significant shift in personality when under high-stress situations (see [[Violet Okonoi#Addendum A|Addendum A]]). Violet also displays anomalous heterochromia, believed to be grace-related.

Violet wields the [[Sympathy]] Blasphemy, granting her a level of attunement with inanimate objects.
