# Personality

Yuki Toriumi was known for being generally aloof, unreadable, and disinterested in social situations. She seldom interacted with her peers and preferred to keep to herself when off-duty, often spending her time tending to her weapons.

Toriumi displayed a marked shift in personality when using her Blasphemy which onlookers described as "psychotic" and "unhinged." She often took sadistic pleasure in the suffering of her targets and would deliberately keep them alive so as to continue inflicting pain[^1].

# Powers and Abilities

Toriumi's Blasphemy, Sever, allows her to cleanly cut any object via mental impulse.
