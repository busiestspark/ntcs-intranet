<div>
	<p>
	Your soles resound through the empty hallway. The only noise besides your careful steps is a gentle breeze wafting in from a nearby window. The sky outside is filled with cotton-candy clouds.
	</p>
	<p>
	Your bag shifts uncomfortably at your back. You remember a great many beautiful days like this, and you know that blue skies aren't always an omen of good fortune.
	</p>
	<p>
	The building is small and you find the room with ease. It's at the end of a dismal hallway with a dying sapling in the corner. A simple, metal placard on the door reads:
	</p>
	<div style="text-align: center">"MR. WHITTAKER"</div>
</div>

---

<div>
	<p>
	"You must be Jesse."
	</p>
	<p>
	A man smiles at you from an armchair across the room. A flannel vest, khaki pants, and a crisp, white undershirt. He's certainly dressed for the part. Kind eyes skim through a dense document behind half-moon glasses.
	</p>
	<p>
	"What brings you in today?" Mr. Whittaker gestures towards an empty chair across from him. "You can leave your things at the door."
	</p>
	<p>
	Dropping your bag, you shuffle towards your seat and make yourself comfortable. The office is quaint, adorned with numerous plants in varying states of hydration. An oak desk sits against one wall, Newton's Cradle hanging undisturbed next to a bulky computer monitor.
	</p>
	<p>
	"I hear you've been having trouble lately up here." Whittaker taps his temple as you sit. You nod, sheepishly.
	</p>
	<p>
	"Well, we'll take care of that." He retrieves a pen from behind his ear and sets himself to write. "Let's begin, then, shall we? I have a few questions for you."
	</p>
	<p>
	Clearing his throat, Whittaker continues. "How long have you been feeling anxious?"
	</p>
	<p>
	About a week, you say.
	</p>
	<p>
	He hums. "Are you still taking your medication?"
	</p>
	<p>
	Of course you have.
	</p>
	<p>
	"I see. Have you been thinking about anything in particular?"
	</p>
	<p>
	You bite the inside of your cheek. That pounding, red memory comes to mind. The one you've been trying to ignore, that curdles in the back of your mind like a fetid stain.
	</p>
	<p>
	It was just a gas leak. That's what you were told.
	</p>
	<p>
	"Gas leaks can cause erratic behaviour, gaps in memory. Do you-"
	</p>
	<p>
	You know that, and you are certain that your memory is not failing.
	</p>
	<p>
	"Alright. It might be difficult, but could you describe what you experienced in detail? Feel free to stop at any point if it feels like too much."
	</p>
</div>

---

<div>
	<p>
	The pungent scent of iron fills the classroom. You sit huddled behind an overturned desk, staring at the periodic table on the far wall. Next to you, stifling a sob, is the girl you came here with. Her face is speckled with blood and both her hands are clasped tightly over her mouth. You try to steady your breathing.
	</p>
	<p>
	"Oh, Katie..." A singsong voice echoes through the room. A shadow dances in your vision, too wild to discern any intelligible shape. You feel the vibrations of heavy footfalls in your bones. Something is breathing hungrily alongside the sickening sound of something heavy being dragged.
	</p>
	<p>
	"Where are you!?" The girl jumps. She suppresses a whimper and, even in the half-light, you can see faint tears roll down her cheeks. Every muscle in your body is quivering. The footfalls continue, the breathing lowering to a near-feral hiss. The shadow grows larger.
	</p>
	<p>
	"There you are." Looming slowly around the corner is something unfathomable. Too many wild, bloodshot eyes dot its face, and a tangle of fleshy strands dance like hair atop its scalp. A horrid grin stretches across its hellish face as it bares its crooked, bloody teeth at its prizes.
	</p>
	<p>
	It's all you can do to run.
	</p>
	<p>
	You think you hear a scream. White hot pain shoots through your arm as you brush past. None of it matters. You need to get out.
	</p>
	<p>
	You scramble through the classroom door and don't stop running until you're halfway home.
	</p>
</div>

---

<div>
	<p>
	"I see." Whittaker's pen hasn't stopped moving since you began. "I think that's all I'll need from you today."
	</p>
	<p>
	What? Wasn't he supposed to help you work through this?
	</p>
	<p>
	"For the record," he pauses to look up at you, eyes suddenly betraying a grim shift in demeanor. "I sincerely apologize for what happens next."
	</p>
	<p>
	You barely have time to process his words before the world goes black.
	</p>
</div>
