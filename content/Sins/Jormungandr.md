# Overview

**Jormungandr** was a Category 7 [[Sins#Centipede|Centipede]] that manifested in downtown Dubai. It was executed during Hunt [[Dubai - 2016-08-09]], also known as the "Dubai Incident."

Unlike most Sins, the events leading to Jormungandr's creation are entirely unknown. It has one of the highest recorded casualty rates of any Sin and is one of the few Sins to have necessitated the deployment of [[Virtues|Virtue]] [[Fortitude]].
