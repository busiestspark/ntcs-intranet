# Overview

**Ebon Moon** is a Category 5 [[Sins#Imago|Imago]] that manifested in downtown Dubai during Hunt [[Dubai - 2016-08-09]].

Ebon Moon manifested from Exorcist [[Yuki Toriumi]] after she suffered terminal [[Sin Overflow]] due to operational stress. Unlike most Imagos, it has shown no signs of attempting to gestate into a true Sin. Its current status remains unknown.
