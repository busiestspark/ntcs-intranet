The **Virtues** are immensely powerful exorcists identifiable by their unique [[Blasphemies#High Blasphemies|High Blasphemies]]. There are currently 6 known Virtues, each possessing a specialized role within the organization.

Unlike most exorcists, the Virtues are capable of sharing their Blasphemies with other users. The nature and effectiveness of this ability varies between Virtues, but it has nevertheless been instrumental in keeping some of them in active duty.
