**Sins** are the primary threats faced by CAIN.

They are noological entities created when a graced individual suffers an intense, traumatic event. Sins are generally hostile and extremely dangerous to human life, making the elimination of them a top priority.

# Basic Traits

Sins can manifest for a wide variety of reasons, but usually emerge due to extreme stress or trauma. The manner of relationship they have with their host is heavily dependent on the type of Sin, and some Sins may even manifest post-mortem.

Every Sin manifests a Palace, an alternate reality that reflects the Sin's desires and beliefs. The Sin is only vulnerable within its Palace, as when fought anywhere else, it will retreat to its Palace to recover. Locating a Sin's Palace is essential to enacting its execution.

Sins are also highly receptive to the traumas that formed them. Sins may reveal weaknesses or vulnerabilities when faced with knowledge of the events that created them; as such, it is standard practice for exorcists to uncover as much information about their target as possible to assist in combating it.

# Forms

Sins may manifest as one of three primary forms.

## Form I/Severed

The most common form for Sins, severed Sins can move entirely independently of their hosts. Its relationship to its host may vary, but they always possess an innate connection. Severed Sins may not require their hosts to be alive and may form post-mortem.

## Form II/Fused

Fused Sins are physically melded to their hosts (or their host's corpse). They often appear humanoid in appearance and are capable of appearing before graceless humans. The host and Sin are effectively a singular entity and typically follow the same goals, although not always. Non-humanoid fused Sins will cause significant cranial trauma to graceless humans that perceive them.

## Form III/Bound

Bound Sins occur when a human or exorcist binds a Form I or II Sin to their service. They do not necessarily need to be the Sin's host.

# Types

Sins are divided into six major classifications based on their traits, abilities, and the events that created them.

Although they may fit into broad categories, no two Sins behave exactly the same, and discretion should be taken to ensure that each one is dispensed with properly.

## Ogre

Ogres are born out of intense despair and hopelessness. They are generally hulking, monstrous, and extremely physically powerful, although they are also unusually conflict-averse. Ogres prefer to isolate and let themselves, their hosts, and the region around them fester in misery.

They are associated with rot, causing ill weather and accelerated decay to occur in areas where they are present.

## Idol

Idols are born out of the intense desire to be wanted. They are generally beautiful and charming in appearance, but are capable of taking more monstrous forms when pressured. Unlike most Sins, Idols are capable of appearing to the graceless without inflicting sever psychic trauma.

Idols are anomalously charismatic and use this ability to charm graceless humans into becoming thrall. They are self-aggrandizing and almost always form cults around themselves.

## Hound

<b>Fact:</b> Hounds are the most likely type of Sin to form post-mortem.
</div>

Hounds are born out of an intense desire for revenge. They extremely violent and animalistic and often take on lithe, animalistic forms. Most Hounds do not possess human-level intelligence, instead operating off of a singular desire to hunt and kill.

As they are based on revenge, Hounds will always hold a grudge against a particular person or group of people. They prioritize this target when hunting, but will seldom pass on another chance to sate their sadistic desires.

## Centipede

<div class="infobox">
<b>Note:</b>Exorcists deployed against Centipedes are required to stock at least one vial of Centipede anti-venom. This vial is single use and should not be wasted on ordinary humans.
</div>

Centipedes are born out of an intense hatred and desire for destruction. They are believed to manifest in individuals who succumb to a deep misanthropy and hatred of the world, but their origins cannot be traced as reliably as other Sins.

Centipedes possess a powerful psycho-biotic venom that mutates ordinary humans into the Centipede's thrall within the space of an hour. This venom can spread through these thrall as well, and as there is no known cure for fully-transformed thrall, they should be considered effectively deceased.

## Toad

Toads are born out of an intense desire for greed and material gain. They are the most intelligent Sins by a wide margin, using this ability to outsmart groups of exorcists and perform grand feats of larceny.

Toads are obsessed with accumulating as much wealth as possible and gain a measure of power from its hoard. Powerful Toads are capable of stealing enormous prizes, including yachts, entire stores, and celebrities.

## Lord

Lords are born out of an intense fear of misfortune and change. They are extremely self-righteous, typically manifesting in those who have suffered something they judge unfair, such as the sudden loss of a loved one.

Lords manifest Kingdoms, alternate realities that spread outward from their palaces and bleed over into our own. Within its Kingdom, the Lord is capable of altering reality in numerous ways, often using this ability to create a comfortable, coddling environment for their host where their losses are amended.

## Imago

Imagos are sins born directly from [[Exorcist|exorcists]] who suffer [[Sin Overflow]]. They are highly intelligent and spiteful, armed with the experience of their time as an exorcist. They are typically motivated to become true Sins by manifesting their own Palace.
