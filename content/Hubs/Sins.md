**Sins** are the primary threats faced by CAIN.

They are noological entities created when a graceful individual suffers an intense, traumatic event. Sins are generally hostile and extremely dangerous to human life, making the elimination of them a top priority.

# Classification

Sins are divided into six major classifications based on their traits, abilities, and the events that created them.

| Sin Type  | Affinity | Behavior and Abilities                             |
| --------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| OGRE      | Despair  | Creates deadly miasma, alters weather patterns     |
| IDOL      | Lust     | Manipulate emotions, forms cults                   |
| HOUND     | Wrath    | Commits and spreads acts of intense violence       |
| CENTIPEDE | Hatred   | Infects humans with psycho-biotic venom            |
| TOAD      | Greed    | Accumulates as much wealth as possible             |
| LORD      | Fear     | Manifests a "Kingdom" where the LORD's word is law |

Although they may fit into broad categories, no two Sins behave exactly the same, and discretion should be taken to ensure that each one is dispensed with properly.
